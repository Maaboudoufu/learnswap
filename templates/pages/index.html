How a swap works

  1. STEP 1

    List both sides

    Say what you can teach and what you want to learn. Both are required — that is the whole idea.

  2. STEP 2

    Get matched both ways

    We only surface people where the trade runs in both directions, so nobody is doing unpaid favors.

  3. STEP 3

    Teach to learn

    Explaining a topic deepens your own grasp of it. The teacher gets as much out of the session as the student.
